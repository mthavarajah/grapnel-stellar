# Bundled asset artwork

All product logo URLs are local. Observed coverage on 2026-11-11: all16Noether underlying symbols, all7Soroswap asset identities (including two explicitly distinct USDC identities), and all46SODAX catalog candidates (42company/equity candidates plus fourETF/fund references). Logos identify a displayed reference; they never establish Testnet deployment, backing, issuer identity, liquidity, eligibility, availability or endorsement. BTC/XRP reference artwork on test tokens does not turn them into native-chain assets.

Existing marks retained unchanged:
- USDC: official Circle [pressroom](https://www.circle.com/pressroom), [brand kit ZIP](https://6778953.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6778953/Pressroom/brandkit/logo-downloads/usdc.zip), Token Logo/USDC Token.svg.
- XLM: [cryptocurrency-icons black mark](https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons/blob/master/svg/black/xlm.svg), CC0 license in `LICENSE-cryptocurrency-icons.md`; [Stellar brand reference](https://stellar.org/brand-resources).

`logo-sources.json` records exact per-file download URLs, rights notices and SHA256 checksums. PNG versions of first-party ICO files preserve their actual image content, selecting the decoder's highest-resolution available frame. No company mark was redrawn or fabricated. Simple Icons and cryptocurrency-icons artwork is CC0 with the copied license texts; brand/trademark rights remain with their owners. First-party favicon/registry artwork and CoinGecko token artwork are retained for referential identification; **no open license is asserted** for those marks. TSMC's wordmark is Commons PD-textlogo, with trademark retained.

PUMP uses the exact Pump.fun token image from public CoinGecko metadata, rather than the unrelated generic documentation favicon. CRCL uses Circle's own website favicon, rather than the similarly named Circle community-platform icon. Hims, Strategy and AMC artwork comes from their linked corporate/investor/store origins. ETF icons identify the named fund manager; they are not fund backing or execution claims. Lululemon's official white favicon is displayed on a neutral dark surface for visibility.

Gold, Silver and Oil are original, code-native category illustrations (`gold.svg`, `silver.svg`, `oil.svg`), not issuer logos. They also cover GC=F, SI=F and CL=F reference symbols. `asset.svg` is an original generic geometric asset icon used only for unknown symbols; there are no colored-letter fallback marks. Original category/generic SVG code follows the repository license.
